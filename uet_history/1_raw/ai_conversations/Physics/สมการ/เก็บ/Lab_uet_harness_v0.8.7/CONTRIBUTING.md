# Contributing to UET Harness

First off, thank you for considering contributing to the Unified Energy Theory (UET) project. Your involvement is key to reaching **Version 1.0.0 (Global Acceptance)**.

## 🌈 The v0.9.x Community Phase
We are currently in Phase 2 of our Roadmap. We welcome:
- **Physics Validation:** New test scripts for specific quantum or astronomical phenomena.
- **Bug Fixes:** Improvements to the PDE solvers or stability scripts.
- **Documentation:** Clarifying physical derivations or improving the Wiki.

## 🚀 How to Contribute

1. **Fork the Repository:** Create your own copy of the harness.
2. **Create a Branch:** `git checkout -b feature/amazing-new-test`.
3. **Write Your Test:** Ensure it follows the structure of `docs/0.8.7/XX-topic/`.
4. **Run the Suite:** Verify that your changes don't break the existing 52 tests (`python run_all_tests.py --full`).
5. **Submit a Pull Request:** Provide a clear description of the physical basis for your contribution.

## ⚖️ Code of Conduct
Please be respectful and professional. We aim for scientific rigor and open, constructive dialogue.
