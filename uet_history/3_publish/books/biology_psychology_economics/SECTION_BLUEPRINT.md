## Section promise

This three-book Section moves from the biological conditions of human survival and perception, through psychology and behavior, into the systemic economic and biopolitical environment that shapes those conditions.

## Three-book architecture

1. Book 1 — Biology: establish the body, brain, energy minimization, and survival mechanisms.
2. Book 2 — Biology and Psychology: connect bodily mechanisms to cognition, identity, behavior, safety, and adaptation.
3. Book 3 — Psychology and Economics: carry the individual and behavioral analysis into systemic incentives, economic organization, and biopolitical effects.

## Boundary

The Section is a working architecture. Each book must keep its own claims, sources, literature review, limitations, and wording boundary. Shared concepts and cross-book handoffs belong in the parent package and must not be silently redefined inside one book.

## Parent-child controls

The Volume Matrix owns primary volume allocation. The Dependency Map owns cross-book prerequisites and handoffs. The Section literature review and claim map must be completed before the parent blueprint is locked under S07.
