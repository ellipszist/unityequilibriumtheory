## Shared research question

How do physical resources, energy services, labor, knowledge, produced capital, institutions,
money, credit, and distribution interact in the creation and allocation of economic output and
wealth; how might AI alter those relations; and what evidence would be required to evaluate a
new monetary-system proposal without presuming that it works?

## Review domains

1. National accounting, comprehensive wealth, welfare, and distribution.
2. Production functions, KLEMS growth accounting, input-output networks, and innovation.
3. Monetary economics, bank money creation, credit, fiscal flows, inflation, and asset pricing.
4. Energy economics, industrial ecology, material flow, exergy, and natural-capital accounting.
5. Labor economics, market power, firm size, time use, and demographic outcomes.
6. Economic history, history of mathematics, archaeology, anthropology, and history of ideas.
7. AI economics, automation, industrial organization, governance, and technology policy.

## Search and appraisal protocol

- Prefer original datasets, official statistical manuals, peer-reviewed primary research, and systematic reviews.
- Record search location, query, search date, screening result, inclusion reason, exact locator, limitations, contrary evidence, and rights status.
- English and Thai sources are in scope. Other languages require a documented translation route.
- Current empirical sources use an explicit retrieval vintage. Historical claims have no date floor but must identify primary records or specialist scholarship.
- Exclude unsourced summaries from evidentiary roles. They may be retained only as search leads.
- A source list does not close S03. The review must synthesize agreement, disagreement, method dependence, uncertainty, and implications for claim wording.

## Shared evidence levels

| Level | Evidence | Maximum permitted claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `L0` | official macro series | descriptive macro association |
| `L1` | sector accounts, KLEMS, I-O, physical coefficients | accounting bridge or modelled footprint |
| `L2` | public partial transaction/firm/household records | bounded transaction evidence |
| `L3` | restricted matched microdata | matched micro evidence under its identification design |
| `L4` | tagged payment, invoice, inventory, and resource ledger | observed lineage within the tagged system only |

Money fungibility is a permanent interpretation constraint. Without a segregated or tagged account, an attribution rule is `ACCOUNTING_INFERRED` or `MODEL_ALLOCATED`, never `OBSERVED`.

## Shared stop rules

- No formula becomes a law through rebasing, fitting, or narrative coherence.
- No M2 correlation becomes a monetary-policy or fiat shock.
- No energy-throughput series becomes literal fuel energy density.
- No historical sequence becomes causal merely because it is chronologically plausible.
- No policy recommendation enters core evidence without a comparison design, distributional outcomes, and rights/ethics review.
