## Current synthesis

### Production and wealth

Standard production accounts distinguish output flows from asset and wealth stocks. KLEMS and input-output approaches treat capital, labor, energy, materials, and services as different inputs rather than dimensions of one additive scalar. Knowledge, institutions, and technology can affect productivity and substitution, but they do not erase physical or ecological constraints. This supports the Section's broad intuition while rejecting `R=N+K+I` as an accounting or physical identity.

### Money, credit, and prices

Money is simultaneously a unit of account, medium of exchange, liquid financial asset, and settlement institution. Commercial-bank lending can create deposits, so money and credit are endogenous to economic decisions. `MV=PY` is an accounting identity when velocity is defined as nominal expenditure divided by a selected money stock; it does not by itself identify the causal effect of money on prices. Purchasing power varies by consumption basket, household, region, and horizon. These findings reject a single observed scalar called “the value of money.”

### Energy, materials, and the environment

Energy throughput, useful energy services, conversion efficiency, EROI, fuel heat content, and exergy are distinct constructs. Industrialization involved fuel, machines, transport, institutions, capital, and knowledge together. Environmental externalities and natural-capital depletion can make GDP an incomplete welfare measure, but accounting profit is not thermodynamic entropy and entropy cannot be subtracted from money without a valuation bridge.

### Labor, distribution, and market power

Productivity-pay gaps depend on sector coverage, mean versus median measures, compensation concepts, deflators, labor share, and distribution. Market concentration, bargaining power, technology, trade, institutions, and housing/health costs are competing mechanisms. Fiat currency alone is not an identified cause. SME employment shares depend on country, year, firm-size definition, and denominator.

### History and anthropology

Control of fire, cooking, storage, settlement, and fuel collection may have co-evolved, but the sequence “food surplus → firewood stock → first industry → first war” is not established by the current evidence package. Claims about mathematics before spoken language, a single origin of geometry, the causal role of Buddhist emptiness in zero, or Crusades as the principal transmission channel require specialist review and remain quarantined.

### AI and monetary design

AI may change productivity, labor demand, market concentration, ownership, and measurement, but those outcomes belong to Book 2. A resource- or energy-linked monetary system is a design proposal requiring simulations of convertibility, oracle governance, volatility, procyclicality, liquidity, distribution, and attack/failure modes; it belongs to Book 3 and cannot be validated by Book 1's descriptive evidence.

## Contrary evidence that changes the architecture

- Constraints can destroy firms, knowledge, infrastructure, and innovation capacity as well as stimulate adaptation.
- Collateral reallocates credit risk but does not guarantee productive output.
- Consumption credit can smooth shocks or finance health and education; “unproductive” cannot be inferred from being unsecured.
- Mining cost does not by itself determine the value of gold or Bitcoin.
- Monetary expansion need not map one-for-one or immediately into consumer prices because money demand, velocity, output, expectations, credit conditions, and supply constraints vary.
- Leisure can increase market services, household production, or neither, depending on income and constraints.
- Fertility change is multicausal; an intent claim about powerful groups is not supported.

## Research implication

The Section retains a biophysical and institutional account of wealth creation, but replaces universal identities with benchmarked hypothesis families. The next review pass must add a search log, source-by-source appraisal, specialist historical review, and exact claim locators before S03 can pass.
