## Section promise

This three-book Section moves from a benchmarked account of production, wealth, money, credit, welfare, and physical constraints; through evidence-based AI divergence scenarios; into an explicitly unvalidated monetary-design proposal with stated failure modes.

## Three-book architecture

1. Book 1 — Economics: define and test production, resources, money, payments, credit, welfare, distribution, market power, energy, and bounded historical claims.
2. Book 2 — Economics and AI: examine how AI and technological divergence may alter production, labor, coordination, and inequality.
3. Book 3 — Economics and Energy: develop the dynamic energy-money proposal and state its evidence boundary, assumptions, and unresolved questions.

## Boundary

The Section is a working architecture. Economic, technological, and monetary proposals remain bounded by their evidence class and must not be presented as validated outcomes without the required review gates.
