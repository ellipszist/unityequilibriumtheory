# Book 1 Source Digest

> **Status:** `IN_PROGRESS`
> **Purpose:** record what each evidence family can and cannot support.

## Production and resources

BEA–BLS KLEMS supplies a standard industry production benchmark. BEA I-O and USEEIO can estimate direct and upstream sector requirements. EIA, USGS, and CFS add physical energy, material, and shipment dimensions. Together they can describe average production networks; they cannot identify which specific borrowed dollar paid which worker or physical input.

## Money, payments, and funding

H.6 supplies money aggregates; Z.1 supplies sectoral balance-sheet and flow accounts; DCPC observes bounded consumer payment behavior; SEC statements show firm cash-flow channels; USAspending shows federal awards, obligations, and some outlays. These sources permit accounting and bounded transaction diagnostics. Money fungibility and missing invoice/settlement links prevent specific-dollar lineage without tagged accounts.

## Prices and welfare

CPI/PCE measure defined price baskets and can be combined with income and expenditure data. They do not create a universal household purchasing-power measure. Regional, income-group, housing, health, food, energy, transport, and time-use results must remain separate where coverage differs.

## Labor and distribution

EPI can be reproduced as its own construction. BLS provides official productivity and compensation measures under different universes and deflators. CPS, CE, QCEW, SCF/DFA, and distributional accounts are required to separate mean, median, labor share, benefits, firm size, and household exposure.

## Energy and environment

EIA throughput is not literal fuel density. Heat content, useful energy, conversion efficiency, EROI, exergy, emissions, and natural-capital depletion need separate units and methodologies. Externality accounting can compare private return and social cost; it does not make profit identical to entropy.

## History

Current institutional and specialist sources support a multicausal account of the Great Depression and caution against single-cause gold narratives. The archaeology, history of mathematics, Newton/Smith, zero-transmission, and firewood claims still require systematic specialist review and remain quarantined.
