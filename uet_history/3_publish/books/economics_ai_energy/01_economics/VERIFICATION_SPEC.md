# Book 1 Verification Specification

## Gate order

1. Section version and ownership alignment.
2. Book ontology, formula, and claim-map alignment.
3. Literature-review and source-provenance readiness.
4. Topic source/panel/artifact integrity.
5. Descriptive and predictive baseline evaluation.
6. Causal identification where explicitly attempted.
7. Independent replication, rights/ethics, and publication review.

## Required machine checks

- Blueprint, manifest, Research Specification, claim map, formula registry, and Topic artifact carry matching version/hash references.
- `BOOK-HEURISTIC-001` remains `RETIRED_AS_IDENTITY`.
- No output calls `R/M` observed money value.
- No transaction result omits its `OBSERVED/ACCOUNTING_INFERRED/MODEL_ALLOCATED/UNOBSERVED` label.
- Global analysis requires every declared variable, non-aggregate country codes, consecutive common coverage, and reconciled downstream counts.
- RMSE, MAE, and median absolute error are not relabelled as one another.
- Bootstrap draws sample blocks with replacement and record seed/block size/draw count.
- Producer artifact hashes must match consumer-embedded hashes.

## Acceptance boundary

Book 1 may report a source-locked descriptive or predictive failure/success under the declared design. It may not claim a universal law, fiat causality, transaction lineage beyond observed data, policy validation, or a validated monetary peg. W05 remains blocked until W03/W04 and the version/ontology gate pass; publication requires W10–W17 and human sign-off.
