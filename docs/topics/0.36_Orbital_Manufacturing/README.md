# 🏗️ 0.36 UET Integrated Shipyard & Orbital Foundry

> **"Forging the Fleet in the Vacuum."**

## 🎯 Problem & Grounded Solution

- **The Problem:** Launching heavy infrastructure from Earth's gravity well is too expensive. Building in space is required, but vacuum welding and heat dissipation are massive hurdles.
- **The Grounded Solution:** **Vacuum Thermodynamics & Automated Shipyards**. UET utilizes the vacuum to its advantage for perfect, oxide-free welding. However, since heat cannot dissipate via convection in space, the foundry focuses on **Radiative Graphene Fins** to vent manufacturing heat, and utilizes ICN (0.34) for zero-gravity atomic assembly of Stingray hulls.
