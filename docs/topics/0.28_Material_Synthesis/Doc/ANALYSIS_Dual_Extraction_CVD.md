## 1. The Economic Scale of Rice Husks
Rice husks are globally abundant. By treating them not as fuel, but as a high-tech precursor, we achieve an unparalleled economic advantage.
- **Carbon:** ~40%. Used for Graphene.
- **Silica:** ~20%. Used for Polysilicon.

## 2. Machine Design: The Dual Foundry
The factory design consists of:
1. **The Leaching Vats:** For base silica separation.
2. **The FJH Capacitor Banks:** To provide the gigawatt-scale microsecond bursts needed to vaporize the carbon.
3. **The Acoustic Chamber:** Where SAW (Surface Acoustic Waves) manipulate the vaporized carbon into the desired lattice.

## 3. Integration with the UET Grid
- **Output to 0.33 (Batteries):** Silicon is used for high-capacity anodes; Graphene for structural electrodes.
- **Output to 0.34 (Nanofabrication):** Silicon chunks are processed into wafers.
- **Output to 0.37 (Solar Paint):** Perovskite synthesis utilizes similar acoustic alignment principles.
