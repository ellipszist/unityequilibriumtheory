# 🚧 System Limitations & Failure Cases
## 1. Thermodynamic Bottlenecks
- **Energy Cost of Flash Joule Heating:** Vaporizing carbon requires immense electrical spikes. The primary limitation is whether the energy required for vaporization costs more than traditional mining methods.
- **Cooling Limits:** Rapidly cooling the CVD chamber without destroying the newly formed graphene lattice is a critical thermal management issue.

## 2. Acoustic Resonance Constraints
- **Substrate Damping:** The acoustic standing wave (SAW) requires a rigid substrate. As the graphene layers grow thicker, the acoustic energy dampens, making it difficult to maintain >99.8% purity for bulk multilayer graphene.
