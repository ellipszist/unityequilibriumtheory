# 🧪 Research Methodology: Dual-Extraction & Acoustic CVD
## 1. Objective
To extract high-purity Carbon and Silicon from agricultural waste (e.g., Rice Husks) and utilize Acoustic CVD to manufacture pristine Graphene, establishing a zero-mining supply chain.

## 2. The 3-Phase Process
### Phase 1: Pyrolysis (Oxygen-free heating)
- **Input:** Raw Rice Husks
- **Process:** Heated to 500-800°C in an inert atmosphere.
- **Output:** Biochar (high carbon + silica content) and volatile syngas.

### Phase 2: Desilication (Chemical Leaching)
- **Process:** Biochar is washed with a mild NaOH solution.
- **Yield A (Silicon):** Silica dissolves into the liquid. It is precipitated and refined into **Polysilicon (Si) chunks**, which are then sent to Topic 0.34 for wafer/nanofabrication.
- **Yield B (Carbon):** The remaining solid is 95-99% pure Carbon powder.

### Phase 3: Acoustic CVD & Flash Joule Heating
- **Process:** The pure Carbon powder undergoes Flash Joule Heating (>3000°C) to vaporize.
- **Acoustic Guidance:** In the CVD chamber, resonant acoustic waves (SAW) guide the carbon atoms to condense into a perfect hexagonal lattice.
- **Output:** Graphene >99.8% purity.
