# 🚧 System Limitations & Failure Cases
## 1. Physical Limitations
- **Ambient Airflow:** Wind speeds above 0.5 m/s will disrupt the acoustic nodes and scatter the perovskite canvas.
- **Acoustic Power:** Generating strong haptic feedback (>50 mN) requires high ultrasonic power, which poses a risk of localized heating or auditory discomfort to animals/humans if safety limits are exceeded.

## 2. Engineering Bottlenecks
- **Refresh Rate:** Moving particles physically to draw 3D shapes is constrained by aerodynamic drag, limiting dynamic animation speed compared to 2D screens.
