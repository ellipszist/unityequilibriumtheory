## 1. Executive Summary
Instead of "freezing light" (which violates physics), Topic 0.27 utilizes **Ultrasonic Phased Arrays** to create localized high-pressure nodes in mid-air. These nodes trap microscopic, bio-safe perovskite particles, forming a physical "scaffolding" upon which RGB lasers project the holographic image.

## 2. Theoretical Framework
### 2.1 Acoustic Radiation Force (ARF)
The levitation of particles is governed by ARF. By modulating the phase of multiple ultrasonic transducers, we control the 3D position of the trapped particles in real-time.
$$ F_{ARF} = \nabla U_{rad} $$
Where $U_{rad}$ is the acoustic radiation potential.

### 2.2 Perovskite as the Canvas
Lead-free perovskite particles are chosen for their high photoluminescence quantum yield (PLQY), converting the projected laser light into vivid, omnidirectional RGB pixels in mid-air.
