# 🔬 ANALYSIS: Tactile Haptics (The "Push")
> **Topic:** 0.27 Cold Light (Hologram)
> **Phenomenon:** Tactile Feedback in Mid-Air
> **Physics:** Ultrasonic Focal Points
> **Status:** 🧪 ACTIVE RESEARCH

## 1. Executive Summary
The sensation of "touching" the hologram is not caused by optical radiation pressure (which is negligible). Instead, the same ultrasonic phased arrays used for levitation are focused directly onto the user's fingertips, creating a localized acoustic pressure that feels physical.

## 2. Theoretical Framework
### 2.1 Focal Pressure Fields
When a user's hand intersects the hologram coordinates (tracked via IR sensors), the ultrasonic array shifts its focal point to the skin surface.
The pressure $P$ at the focal point creates a macroscopic force:
$$ F = P \cdot A_{contact} $$
This achieves the "Solid Light" tactile illusion entirely within the laws of wave mechanics, requiring no magical "virtual mass" from photons.
