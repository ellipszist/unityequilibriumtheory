## Preprocessing

- The primary verifier converts measured force from pN to nN using `1 pN = 0.001 nN`.
- The engine converts separation from nm to m and sphere radius from um to m.
- The current verifier uses a model radius of `200 um`; the dataset declares `196 um`. This is recorded as a limitation and should become a sensitivity run.
- Topic-level source-evidence and branch-claim gate files keep the accepted Casimir benchmark separate from blocked vacuum-energy and cosmology claims.

## Data Policy

- Raw external archival material, when added, should be stored under `docs/data/external/...`.
- Topic-specific derived or normalized data remains under `docs/topics/0.12_Vacuum_Energy_Casimir/Data/...`.
- No downstream topic may treat the dark-energy anchor as data-derived from this manifest.
