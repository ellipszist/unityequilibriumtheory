# UET Foundation-First Research Program — Waves 0–10

## Purpose

This report is the control surface for the current foundation program. It does
not replace the lane artifacts. Its job is to preserve the order of work:

```text
inventory → ontology → standard correspondence → units → derivation
→ formal verification → numerical verification → observable mapping
→ real-data comparison → claim
```

The machine-readable status is generated by
`docs/scripts/audit/audit_uet_wave3_wave10_program.py` and written to
`docs/core/artifacts/uet_wave3_wave10_research_program.json`.

## Named persistence principle

The persistence-energy diagnostic is now organized under
UET-PRINCIPLE-001 ? Cooperative Energy Allocation for System Persistence
Principle. It is a result-based selection hypothesis: persistent patterns may
be the patterns whose interaction-related resource use does not exhaust their
declared persistence capacity too quickly. It does not assign intent to matter
and does not identify the ledger with total or potential energy.

The current operationalization is a normalized Rayleigh-type path-cost
comparator. Its artifact is SIMULATION_ONLY and its controlling blocker is a
physical mapping from path cost to measured work, heat, flux, or entropy
production in a declared lane.

## Current decision

The program remains BLOCKED. This is a dependency result, not a statement
that every local calculation failed.

- The conserved-C lane is retained as a normalized phase/order comparator. Its
  changing-C gradient dynamics must not be described as a finite-cone law.
- The selected non-conserved characteristic lane now passes its declared
  normalized compact-support and ledger contract, and is reachable through the
  explicit UETMasterEquation adapter. This is still a simulation-only candidate,
  not a physical causality proof.
- The original default/full nonlinear candidate remains blocked: its recorded
  pre-arrival leakage is 1.76394e-2 against the 1e-6 threshold.
- The persistence dynamic-selection and normalized thermal bridge now close
  internal resource/work ledgers, but their physical work/heat/temperature/
  entropy mapping remains open.
- Wave 4-10 work is therefore retained as diagnostic, comparator, or
  dependency-gated research. A downstream local PASS cannot override a
  blocked foundation gate.

## Claim ceiling

The current allowed language is `candidate collective-behaviour coordinate`,
`candidate normalized effective model`, `internal diagnostic`,
`simulation/comparator contract`, `tree-level finite-density O(2) EOS`, and
`covariant ideal-superfluid constitutive structure` where the corresponding
artifact says so.

The program does not establish that (C) is universal mass, that (R_{gen})
is a new substance, that (Phi) is a metric or particle, that a photon or
neutrino is (R_{gen}), that UET derives GR, that the global universe is open,
or that UET replaces dark matter.
