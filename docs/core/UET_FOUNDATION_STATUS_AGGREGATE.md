## Working principles

- Separate state variables, response variables, derived traces, and observables.
- A force is variational only when it is the derivative of the declared functional.
- `C` has no universal physical identity; mass, density, charge, and order parameter require
  lane-specific mappings.
- An open-system balance applies to an explicitly defined effective subsystem, not
  automatically to the universe as a whole.
- A special case requires an explicit limit, compatible ontology and units, and residual or
  convergence evidence.
- Free-energy descent, physical energy conservation, entropy production, and open exchange
  are separate ledgers.
- No real-data claim is permitted before an observable operator, units, uncertainty, and
  provenance contract exist.
