# Theory Lineage and Sources

This note collects the most important “where did this theory come from?” material that used
to be scattered through legacy theory maps and conceptual framework notes.

## The intended lineage

UET draws from several major scientific traditions at once.

### 1. Thermodynamics

UET treats equilibrium, dissipation, exchange, and irreversible processes as foundational
language rather than as late-stage applications.

This is why many UET terms are written as costs, gradients, exchange flows, and stability
penalties.

### 2. Information theory and Landauer

UET assumes that information is physical and that changing, storing, erasing, or addressing
information carries energetic meaning.

This is one of the most distinctive ingredients in the project and a major reason the
framework leans toward an information-thermodynamic worldview.

### 3. Einstein / relativity

UET inherits the idea that geometry and physical dynamics are deeply linked.

In the legacy theory map, relativity appears as one branch of a deeper root rather than as
something being discarded.

### 4. Quantum mechanics

UET treats quantum behavior as part of the same larger system, often reframed in terms of
state density, addressing, search, or geometric information structure.

This does not automatically mean the quantum sector is fully derived, but it does explain
why quantum concepts appear early in the theory language.

### 5. Newtonian mechanics

Newton remains important as a classical limit and reduction target.

This is a strength of the project's method: it does not treat classical mechanics as
obsolete, but as a constraint that any broader theory should recover in the appropriate
regime.

## Why the lineage matters

The theory is easiest to misunderstand if someone reads only the boldest old claims and
misses this structure.

The main idea is not “replace all prior theories by brute force.”

The main idea is:

- identify shared structure across major theories
- express that structure in one candidate framework
- test whether familiar theories reappear as limits, projections, or branches

## The current scientific caution

The lineage is conceptually strong, but conceptual lineage is not the same as finished
derivation.

At the current project stage:

- some links are philosophically clear but mathematically incomplete
- some benchmark successes support the framework indirectly
- some bridge terms remain heuristic rather than first-principles closed

So the safe claim is that UET is a candidate synthesis framework with explicit roots in
these traditions, not that all of them have already been fully derived from one equation.
