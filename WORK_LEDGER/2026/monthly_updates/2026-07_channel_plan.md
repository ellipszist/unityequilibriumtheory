Status: `planning`

new claims. `Threads` is the frequent research pulse surface; Instagram Stories can reshare
approved updates; the other channels receive the monthly or milestone versions.

## Instagram / Threads operating rule

Threads can carry short research updates during the month, but only from actual weekly pulse
records. Instagram Stories can reshare an approved Feed or Threads item; Stories should not
become a second source of truth. A missing pulse means no forced post.
